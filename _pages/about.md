I am a Research Scientist at [Google Cloud AI Research](https://research.google/teams/cloud-ai-research/), working on **AI security** and **trustworthy LLM agents**. I received a dual PhD in Computer Science and Statistics from Michigan State University, where I was advised by [Prof. Jiliang Tang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=WtzKMWAAAAAJ&hl=en) (Computer Science) and [Prof. Yuehua Cui](https://www.stt.msu.edu/~cui/) (Statistics).

As large language models evolve into autonomous agents that reason, plan, and act through external tools, they bring new kinds of security and reliability risks. My research aims to understand these risks and to build agentic AI systems that can operate safely in the real world:

- **Uncovering vulnerabilities.** I study how LLMs and agents fail, including **jailbreaks** ([EMNLP 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.401.pdf)), **malicious message flow between agents** ([ACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.349.pdf)), **agent memory privacy** ([ACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1227.pdf)), and **memory injection attacks** ([NeurIPS 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.03704)). I also build automated red-teaming and evaluation frameworks ([EACL 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.07505)) to expose these weaknesses systematically.
- **Securing agents.** I develop lightweight defenses that use an LLM's own **attention signals to monitor inter-agent message flow**, assessing whether each message can be trusted before agents act on it ([NeurIPS 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.02546)).
- **Reasoning, interpretability, and tool use.** I study **how models reason** ([EMNLP 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.19000), [Stat](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/sta4.70045), [AISTATS](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.16540)), **how their behaviors can be interpreted and controlled** ([NeurIPS 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.06632)), and **how they use tools** ([ICLR 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.04550)).

My path into this area began with the **robustness of deep learning models**, through work on adversarial attacks ([ICML 2023](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/xu23e/xu23e.pdf)) and data poisoning ([ICLR 2024](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.14851), [TMLR 2024](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=Flh5EXz8dA)). That work laid the foundation for my current research on LLM and agent security. Looking ahead, my goal is to develop principled foundations and evaluation methods for safe and reliable agentic AI.

I enjoy working across disciplines, especially on deploying AI safely in high-impact domains such as **education** ([TALE 2025](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/stamp/stamp.jsp?arnumber=11346606), [EMNLP 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.03090)), **cybersecurity** ([ICML 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.02164)), and **scientific discovery**. Feel free to reach out if you would like to chat or collaborate!

When I am not doing research, I am usually being supervised by my two cats, Fisher and Huli ([meet them here](/life/)).
