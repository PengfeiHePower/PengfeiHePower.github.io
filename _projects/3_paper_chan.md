Paper-chan is an LLM-powered personal research assistant for keeping up with new papers and talks.

- **Daily digest**: a morning summary of papers matching your topics, with recommendations and source links.
- **Content discovery**: blogs, talks, and podcasts from RSS, YouTube, and curated feeds.
- **Telegram bot**: real-time Q&A, on-demand search, and reading-list management.
- **Reading list**: save, tag, and track papers you have read or want to read.
